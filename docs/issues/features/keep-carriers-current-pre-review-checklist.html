Before you start

  • Build from the branch, and keep mekhq.log open alongside — several checks are "and nothing was logged as an error".
  • Two saves are useful: a fresh command generated on this branch, and the Violence as a Service31510108_Cooker save from issue #9880, which predates the carrier flag and exercises the migration path.
  • The TOE tab is where most of this is read. Support Command hangs off the campaign HQ formation.
  • Four checks are marked gate (03, 04, 14, 16). If any of those fails, the PR does not open — they cover new persisted state and permanent data loss.

Generation still works

01 regression
Do Start a new campaign, New Player Preset, and run the Command Generator through to Accept & Build.
Expect Support Command appears under HQ with Maintenance, Medical and Command sections, profession companies beneath them, and carriers under those — exactly as on main. Fails if: the structure is missing, duplicated, or staff are left loose in the roster.
02 new
Do On the campaign from check 01, count the carriers and compare with a generation on main using the same preset and seed if you can hold it steady.
Expect The same carriers, at the same sizes. The generator recruits hundreds of people and each fires an event, so this is the check that the hook is not seating people one at a time ahead of organize(). Log: between [SupportTOE] organize START and [SupportTOE] organized support staff there must be no Seated or Built support carrier lines. Fails if: there are noticeably more, smaller carriers than on main, or duplicate profession companies.

The new persisted state

03 gate
Do Save the generated campaign, exit to the start screen, and load it again.
Expect Support Command and all its carriers come back unchanged, and no duplicate carriers or formations appear on load. Optionally unzip the save and confirm <carrier>true</carrier> on the carrier units and one <supportCommandFormationId> on the campaign. Fails if: carriers lose their flag, the structure duplicates, or the load throws.
04 gate
Do Load Violence as a Service31510108_Cooker, which was saved before the carrier flag existed.
Expect It loads without error. Log: Marked N pre-existing support carrier(s) during load, then Support carrier reconciliation: seated N, released N, removed N empty carrier(s), reshaped N profession(s) with a seated count near 100 for the loose administrators in that save, preceded by the individual Seated / Built support carrier lines. Fails if: the load throws, ordinary infantry gets marked as a carrier, or nothing is seated.
05 new
Do Save the migrated campaign from check 04, then load it again.
Expect The second load logs exactly Support carrier reconciliation: seated 0, released 0, removed 0 empty carrier(s), reshaped 0 profession(s) and no Re-packed lines. The line is always written, so its absence is itself a failure. Fails if: carriers keep being created on every load.

Coming and going

06 new
Do Find a profession carrier that is understrength, then hire one person of that exact role.
Expect They appear in that carrier in the TOE immediately, with no new unit created. Log: one Seated NAME in support carrier UNIT (n/m) line and no Built support carrier. Fails if: a second carrier is built while one had a free seat.
07 new
Do Keep hiring that same role until every carrier of it is full, then hire one more.
Expect The profession is re-packed to what generation would build for the new total, under the same profession formation, and the newcomer is in it. Log: a Re-packed line with built 1 and a small moved, followed by a Seated line for the newcomer. Fails if: a carrier is created per recruit, or the new one lands outside the profession formation.
08 new
Do Take a profession sitting at, say, 10 people in a Support Squad (7 person) plus a Support Squad (3 person). Hire 32 more of that role in one bulk hire.
Expect 42 people is exactly what generation would pack as one Support Platoon (28 person) plus two Support Squad (7 person) — so that is what the TOE shows at the end, and the 7+3 that were there are gone. Intermediate shapes along the way are fine; the final one must match. Log: a Re-packed ... kept K, built B, removed R, moved M line on most hires while the tail squad is filling — that is expected, the tail squad file changes size — but moved must stay small (at most six) on every one of them except the single promotion to a platoon, where about 25 move once. kept should equal the number of platoons already there. Fails if: the final shape is not 28 + 7 + 7, the old 3-person unit survives, the profession formation is deleted mid-rebuild, or any Re-packed line reports moved in the dozens outside the platoon promotion — that means the platoons are being rebuilt instead of kept.
09 new
Do The issue #9880 repro: bulk hire 100 administrators in one go, then look at the TOE and the log.
Expect All 100 end up seated. Carrier count grows by roughly one per seven people, and no carrier holds more than its capacity. The hire completes without a visible stall. Log: Re-packed lines on many of the hires are fine; what matters is that kept counts the existing platoons and moved stays under seven except at each platoon promotion. If moved is in the hundreds, whole platoons are being rebuilt. Fails if: the UI hangs, a carrier is overfilled, or 100 carriers appear.
10 new
Do Bring a profession to an exact multiple of seven — 14 doctors as two full Support Squad (7 person) — then hire one more, then a few more.
Expect The fifteenth person triggers Re-packed ... kept 2, built 1, removed 0, moved 0 and is then Seated in the new Support Squad (2 person). Each further hire seats or reshapes normally. This is the case that failed in play: a carrier built empty was invisible to the seat loop, so the newcomer stayed loose and every following event built another empty carrier. Fails if: the newcomer is not seated, the WARN no ... carrier has a free seat, although the shape was sized for them appears, or empty Support Squad (2 person) units pile up in the TOE.
11 new
Do Take a MekWarrior with no unit assignment and change their primary role to Administrator.
Expect They appear in a Command section carrier without any further action. Log: a Seated or Built support carrier line naming them. Fails if: nothing happens, or they are pulled out of a fighting unit to be seated.
12 new
Do Take a technician sitting in a carrier and change their primary role to MekWarrior.
Expect They leave the carrier. This is the one departure the campaign engine did not already handle, so it is the check most likely to catch a regression later. Log: Releasing NAME from carrier UNIT: no longer carried by this profession. Fails if: they stay listed in the MekTech carrier with a warrior's role.
13 new
Do Find or make a carrier with very few crew, then retire or GM-kill every one of them.
Expect Seats vacate as each person goes, and when the loss crosses a packing boundary the profession re-packs downward — a platoon that drops far enough becomes squads. When the last person of a profession leaves, the carrier disappears, and if it was the only carrier in its profession formation that formation goes too. Support Command and its sections stay. Log: Re-packed at boundaries, Removing empty support carrier UNIT, then Removing empty support formation NAME only when the formation was left empty. Fails if: an empty carrier lingers, an empty profession formation lingers, or Support Command itself is deleted.
14 gate
Do Pick a seated support character and use GM Remove from the personnel table to delete them from the roster. Do it to a few. Then save, exit, and reload.
Expect They vanish from the carrier and stay gone. Log: no Seated line for the removed name after the removal, and on the reload no references missing driver errors. This is a distinct code path from death or retirement — removePerson rather than changeStatus — and in play it re-seated the person being deleted and left a ghost crew reference in the save. Fails if: a Seated line names the removed person, the carrier still counts them, or the reload logs Unit ... references missing driver.
15 new
Do Send a seated technician to an academy or set them On Leave, confirm the seat vacates, then return them to Active.
Expect They leave the carrier on departure and are seated again on return — not necessarily in the same carrier, which is expected. Log: a Seated line on return. Fails if: they come back Active and stay outside the TOE.

Not deployable, and duties untouched

16 gate
Do Assign a profession company (or HQ, which carries Support Command with it) to a scenario so its carriers show as deployed. While deployed: hire one of that profession, GM-remove a seated one, and retrain a seated one out. Then resolve or clear the deployment.
Expect Carriers deploy like any other unit — that is deliberate; a base attack may pull support staff into a fight. While deployed, nothing moves: the hire logs Not seating NAME: a LABEL carrier is deployed, and the removal and retrain change nothing in the carrier. When the deployment ends, Deployment changed: reshaped N support profession(s) appears if the count crossed a boundary, and the loose hire is seated on their next event. Fails if: a carrier is refused deployment, anything is seated into or reshaped around a deployed carrier, or the profession stays misshapen after the deployment ends.
17 regression
Do Note maintenance capacity, doctor capacity and the administrator count before and after a batch of hires get seated.
Expect All three rise by the full amount hired. Being in a carrier must not remove anyone from the maintenance pool, the medical pool or the admin headcount — this is the invariant the whole feature rests on. Fails if: capacity does not move when support staff are seated.
18 new
Do Start a campaign without running the Command Generator, or load an old save that never had one. Hire a technician and an administrator.
Expect Nothing is created. No Support Command appears, no carriers are built, and the hires sit in the roster as they always did. Log: on load, Support carrier reconciliation: no Support Command formation, so carriers are not managed in this campaign, and no Seated or Built lines after the hires. Fails if: a support structure springs into existence in a campaign that never opted in.
