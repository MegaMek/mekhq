Windchild's Guide to Loading Portraits in MekHQ
Written 08-Feb-2020, Updated 05-Aug-2020, 01-Nov-2021, 17-Aug-2026.
MekHQ version 0.51.01


When Does MekHQ Assign a Random Portrait?
Random portraits are only handed out when the campaign options allow it. The relevant options live under
Campaign Options -> Name and Portrait Generation Options:
- The "Portrait Assignment" section holds a checkbox per primary role. If the box for a character's PRIMARY role is
  off, that character is never given a random portrait, no matter what is in the folders. Only MekWarrior is ticked by
  default, so this is usually the first thing to change.
- "No Random Portraits for Children" (default ON) skips children entirely. If "Generate Random Portraits for Children
  Coming of Age" (default ON) is also set, those characters get a portrait when they turn 16, provided they still have
  the default portrait at that point.
- "Allow Duplicate Portraits" (default ON) lets a portrait be reused. With it off, any portrait already assigned to
  another character in the campaign is removed from the pool before the random pick.
- "Portraits Ignore Roles" (default OFF) skips the role and role-group folders completely, going straight to the
  Combat/Support step and then the gendered folder.


How Portrait Loading Works:
MekHQ first determines the character's gender, then walks the following folders in order:
    1. /<Gender>/<Primary Role Label>       e.g. /Male/MekWarrior, /Female/Vehicle Crew/Ground
    2. /<Gender>/<Role Group>               e.g. /Male/Admin, /Female/Tech (only some roles have a group; see below)
    3. /<Gender>/Combat or /<Gender>/Support
    4. /<Gender>                            everything for that gender

The first folder in that list which contains at least one eligible picture wins, and a random picture is chosen from
that folder. The search then stops. So if you put pictures in both /Male/MekWarrior and /Male/Combat, Male MekWarriors
will never be assigned one of the Combat pictures unless every Male MekWarrior-specific picture is already taken.

Subfolders count. As of 0.51.01 a folder search matches that folder AND everything nested underneath it. This has two
consequences worth knowing:
- A folder can be organised into subfolders however you like. /Male/Admin covers /Male/Admin/Veterans, and so on.
- The final /<Gender> step therefore covers every portrait filed anywhere under that gender. In practice a character
  will nearly always end up with something rather than the default silhouette.
Pictures sitting loose in the top-level portraits folder (such as default.gif) are outside every gendered folder and
are never picked at random.


Where the Portraits Live:
Two locations are read and merged together:
- <MekHQ install directory>/data/images/portraits
- <your MegaMek user directory>/data/images/portraits

The user directory is the better home for your own portraits: it is set in MegaMek's client settings, and its contents
survive upgrading MekHQ. Story arcs may also ship their own data/images/portraits folder, which is merged in as well.

The paths below are all relative to one of those two portraits folders.

For example, to load a group of portraits only for Male MekWarriors, the folder is:
    <portraits>/Male/MekWarrior
For a portrait usable by any Female administrator:
    <portraits>/Female/Administrator
For a portrait usable by any Female technician (MekTech, Mechanic, AeroTek or Battle Armor Tech):
    <portraits>/Female/Tech


Gendered Folders: All of the folders below MUST sit inside one of these two, or the portraits will not be loaded.
Female characters:              /Female
Everyone else:                  /Male

contains a "/", that is a real nested folder: the Vehicle Crew/Ground role reads from /<Gender>/Vehicle Crew/Ground.

All civilian roles share a single /Civilian folder. That covers Dependent, None, and the whole list of civilian
professions (Chef, Broker, Noble, and the rest), plus any role that has since been deprecated. There is no folder for
an individual civilian profession, and there are no longer separate /Dependent or /None folders.


Role Group Folders:
These are a fallback for when the role folder above is missing or exhausted. Only the roles listed here have one.
Administrators:                 /Admin           Administrator
Vehicle Crews:                  /Vehicle Crew    Vehicle Crew/Ground, Vehicle Crew/Naval, Vehicle Crew/VTOL
Vessel Crewmembers:             /Vessel Crew     Vessel Pilot, Vessel Gunner, Vessel Crewmember, Hyperspace Navigator
Techs:                          /Tech            MekTech, Mechanic, AeroTek, Battle Armor Tech
Medical Staff:                  /Medical         Doctor, Medic

Two traps here:
- /Vessel Crew applies only to the Vessel Crewmember role. Vessel Pilots, Vessel Gunners and Hyperspace Navigators do
  NOT fall back to it; they go straight from their own role folder to /Combat.
- Astechs have no group folder. They go from /Astech straight to /Support.

This step predates the current portrait folders and is kept so that collections sorted by role family keep working. The
stock portraits are all filed under their own role folder, so nothing shipped depends on it. The one you are most
likely to have is /<Gender>/Admin, which is where administrator portraits shipped before 0.51.01; it still works, and
still feeds every administrator.


General Grouping Folders:
Combat Personnel:               /Combat
Support Personnel:              /Support

Combat covers every combat role: MekWarrior, LAM Pilot, the three Vehicle Crew roles, Aerospace Pilot, Conventional
Aircraft Crew, ProtoMek Pilot, Battle Armor Pilot, Soldier, Vessel Pilot, Vessel Gunner, Vessel Crewmember and
Hyperspace Navigator. Everything else -- support staff and civilians alike -- uses Support.


Post-0.51.01 Compatibility:
The four administrator roles were merged into a single Administrator role. Admin/Command, Admin/Logistical,
Admin/Transport and Admin/HR no longer exist as assignable roles, and all administrators now read from
/<Gender>/Administrator, falling back to /<Gender>/Admin.

The stock portraits moved to match. The four /<Gender>/Admin/{Command,HR,Logistical,Transport} folders are gone and
their portraits now sit in /<Gender>/Administrator.

Existing campaigns are migrated for you and need no attention:
- Characters keep the portrait they already had. Loading a campaign repoints any portrait that referenced one of the
  four old sub-folders at /<Gender>/Administrator, and the new path is written back out the next time the campaign is
  saved. The picture itself is unchanged.
- A character keeps the old administrator role only until the save is opened; MekHQ converts them to Administrator on
  load.

Your own portraits are not touched. If you had built your own /<Gender>/Admin folder, it keeps its name, keeps its
contents, and keeps feeding administrators through the role group fallback. Only the four folder names that shipped
with MekHQ are repointed, so sorting your own portraits into an /Admin folder still works exactly as before.


Post-0.50.10 Compatibility:
The vehicle crew update replaced the old vehicle roles and, with them, their portrait folders.

Old folder                      New folder
/Vehicle Driver                 /Vehicle Crew/Ground
/Naval Driver                   /Vehicle Crew/Naval
/VTOL Pilot                     /Vehicle Crew/VTOL
/Vehicle Gunner                 /Vehicle Crew/Ground  (the Vehicle Gunner role was removed)
/Vehicle Crewmember             /Vehicle Crew/Generic (no role targets this folder directly any more; it is reached
                                                       through the /Vehicle Crew group fallback)

For existing campaigns you will need to rename your folders to match the new format. Anything left in an old folder
name is not lost -- it still sits under /<Gender>, so it can be picked by the final fallback step -- but it will no
longer be tied to the role you filed it under.
